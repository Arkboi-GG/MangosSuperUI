# WorldSession

`WorldSession` is the central abstraction representing a single authenticated connection between a client (or bot) and the WoW server. It manages the entire lifecycle of a user session, from initial authentication through gameplay to logout and cleanup, acting as the primary dispatcher for all network opcodes received from that connection.

## How the class is split

The `WorldSession` class is implemented across numerous translation units, each handling a specific domain of gameplay or system interaction. The partials are organized by functional responsibility:

*   **WorldSession.Main**: Manages the core session lifecycle, including packet I/O, state tracking (logged in, transferring, disconnected), player association, security/anti-cheat integration, and account data persistence. It also provides the infrastructure for bot sessions that simulate players without a physical network socket.
*   **WorldSession.AuctionHouseHandler**: Handles all network packets related to the Auction House, including listing items, placing bids, canceling auctions, and sending notifications or mail for auction outcomes.
*   **WorldSession.BattleGroundHandler**: Processes client requests for Battleground (BG) interactions, such as joining queues, teleporting to BG instances, and updating status. It validates player eligibility and coordinates with `BattleGroundMgr`.
*   **WorldSession.ChannelHandler**: Implements logic for chat channel management, including joining, leaving, moderating, and configuring channels. It routes requests to the `ChannelMgr` and handles cross-faction GM access.
*   **WorldSession.CharacterHandler**: Manages the core character lifecycle, including enumeration, creation, deletion, and the complex asynchronous loading of a character’s full state from the database into the game world.
*   **WorldSession.ChatHandler**: Processes all player-initiated communication, including chat messages, emotes, and social interactions. It enforces input sanitization, anti-spam measures, and message routing.
*   **WorldSession.CombatHandler**: Handles combat-related opcodes, such as initiating melee attacks, stopping attacks, and changing weapon sheath states. It validates targets and synchronizes attack states with the client.
*   **WorldSession.DuelHandler**: Processes duel acceptance and cancellation opcodes, validating participant states and triggering duel resolution logic.
*   **WorldSession.GMTicketHandler**: Handles GM Ticket system packets, including creating, viewing, updating, and deleting tickets, as well as submitting player surveys. It delegates lifecycle management to `TicketMgr`.
*   **WorldSession.GroupHandler**: Manages group and raid operations, including invitations, membership changes, leader transfers, and state synchronization (loot methods, ready checks, stats).
*   **WorldSession.GuildHandler**: Processes guild-related opcodes, including creation, membership management, rank changes, and information queries. It validates permissions and delegates state changes to `GuildMgr`.
*   **WorldSession.ItemHandler**: Handles item-related interactions, including inventory manipulation, equipment, bank access, and vendor transactions. It validates client requests and delegates state changes to the `Player` class.
*   **WorldSession.LFGHandler**: Implements logic for Meeting Stone interactions, allowing players or groups to join the Dungeon Finder (LFG) queue. It validates eligibility and delegates queue management to `LFGMgr`.
*   **WorldSession.LootHandler**: Processes loot-related opcodes, validating player eligibility, distributing items and gold, and updating the state of looted objects.
*   **WorldSession.MailHandler**: Manages the in-game mail system, including sending, receiving, reading, and deleting mail. It handles persistence and asynchronous queries for mailbox capacity.
*   **WorldSession.MiscHandler**: Handles miscellaneous opcodes that do not fit into dedicated subsystems, including player lifecycle events (respawn, logout), social interactions (Who List, Friends), world interactions (area triggers), and administrative tools.
*   **WorldSession.MovementHandler**: Processes movement-related packets, validating coordinates, handling teleportation orchestration, managing mover authority, and synchronizing movement states. It integrates with `MovementAnticheat` for cheat detection.
*   **WorldSession.NPCHandler**: Manages interactions with Non-Player Characters (NPCs), including trainers, bankers, tabard vendors, spirit healers, innkeepers, and stable masters. It validates interactions and processes transactions.
*   **WorldSession.PetHandler**: Handles pet-related interactions, including actions, action bar management, renaming, abandonment, and talent resetting. It validates requests and delegates to `Pet` and `CharmInfo` subsystems.
*   **WorldSession.PetitionsHandler**: Implements the Guild Charter system, handling the acquisition, management, signing, and completion of guild petitions. It validates names, funds, and faction restrictions.
*   **WorldSession.QueryHandler**: Processes client queries for static or semi-static metadata, such as player names, creature templates, game object details, and NPC dialogue text. It retrieves data from `ObjectMgr` or live `Player` objects.
*   **WorldSession.QuestHandler**: Handles quest-related packets, including querying availability, accepting quests, tracking progress, completing objectives, and claiming rewards. It validates interactions and coordinates with `Player` and `ScriptMgr`.
*   **WorldSession.SkillHandler**: Processes skill and talent-related opcodes, including learning talents, wiping talents, and unlearning skills. It validates requests and delegates to the `Player` class.
*   **WorldSession.SpellHandler**: Provides network-layer entry points for spell-related actions, including item usage, spell casting, interruption, and pet spell management. It validates opcodes and delegates to `Spell`, `Player`, and `Unit` subsystems.
*   **WorldSession.TaxiHandler**: Implements the Taxi (Flight Path) system, handling node discovery, menu generation, and flight initiation. It relies on `PlayerTaxi` for node knowledge and cost calculations.
*   **WorldSession.TradeHandler**: Manages player-to-player trading, including initiation, negotiation, acceptance, execution, and cancellation. It enforces security rules and coordinates `TradeData` states.

## How the partials collaborate

The `WorldSession` class acts as a central hub, with `WorldSession.Main` providing the foundational infrastructure for packet processing and state management. All other partials rely on `WorldSession.Main` to access the associated `Player` object (`GetPlayer`), account information (`GetAccountId`, `GetSecurity`), and network socket (`GetSocket`).

*   **Packet Dispatch**: Incoming packets are parsed by `WorldSession.Main` and dispatched to the appropriate handler method in the relevant partial based on the opcode.
*   **State Validation**: Most partials perform initial validation (e.g., checking if the player is alive, in combat, or has sufficient permissions) before delegating complex logic to specialized managers (e.g., `AuctionHouseMgr`, `BattleGroundMgr`, `GuildMgr`, `LootMgr`).
*   **Database Interaction**: While some partials (like `WorldSession.CharacterHandler` and `WorldSession.MailHandler`) perform direct database queries, most rely on the `Player` object or manager classes to handle persistence. `WorldSession.Main` handles account-specific data persistence (tutorials, global cache).
*   **Bot Support**: `WorldSession.Main` supports bot sessions by allowing the creation of sessions without a physical network socket. Bot-specific AI units (e.g., `AiBotAI`, `PlayerBotAI`) interact with the session via `QueuePacket` and `OnPacketReceived` hooks, simulating client behavior.
*   **Anti-Cheat Integration**: `WorldSession.Main` integrates with the `Anticheat` system, providing hooks for cheat detection and enforcement. Many partials call `ProcessAnticheatAction` to log suspicious activities.

## Data model

The `WorldSession` class interacts with several database tables, primarily through the `characters` and `account` databases:

*   **`account`**: Stores account-specific data, including security levels, session keys, and flags. Accessed by `WorldSession.Main` for authentication and session management.
*   **`account_data`**: Stores persistent account-specific data (e.g., macros, talent trees) loaded and saved by `WorldSession.Main`.
*   **`character_tutorial`**: Stores tutorial flags for each account, managed by `WorldSession.Main`.
*   **`characters`**: Stores core character data (name, race, class, level, position, etc.), accessed by `WorldSession.CharacterHandler` during login and creation.
*   **`character_account_data`**: Stores character-specific account data, updated by `WorldSession.Main`.
*   **`auction`**: Stores active auctions, accessed by `WorldSession.AuctionHouseHandler` for bidding and buyout operations.
*   **`mail`** and **`mail_items`**: Store mail messages and attached items, accessed by `WorldSession.MailHandler` for sending and receiving mail.
*   **`character_gifts`**: Stores wrapped gift items, accessed by `WorldSession.ItemHandler` and `WorldSession.SpellHandler` for wrapping and opening gifts.
*   **`character_pet`**: Stores pet data, accessed by `WorldSession.PetHandler` for renaming pets.
*   **`gm_surveys`** and **`gm_subsurveys`**: Store GM ticket survey data, accessed by `WorldSession.GMTicketHandler` for submitting surveys.

Most gameplay state (inventory, spells, quests, etc.) is managed by the `Player` object and persisted via its methods, rather than being accessed directly by `WorldSession` partials.
